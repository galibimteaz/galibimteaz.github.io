The Domain Guy Who Actually Shows the Math

Domain Decoded isn't a guesswork listing site. Every domain shown here — before it's ever published — goes through the same five-stage due-diligence process. This page explains what that actually means.

What I Actually Do

I'm Imteaz (Galib) Chowdhury — a domain investor and naming strategist. I acquire, screen, and hold premium .com domains across LegalTech, FinTech, Energy, brandable, and content-brand categories, then make them available to founders who'd rather buy a clean, ready-to-build name than spend months workshopping one in-house.

"Comparable sales first, pricing second" isn't just a tagline — it's the actual order of operations behind every listing on this site.

The Process Behind Every Listing

No domain gets added to the portfolio on gut feeling. Every name is screened through DIOS (Domain Investing Operating System) — a personal gate-and-score framework built specifically for this business: a binary reject gate first, then an 11-factor weighted score for whatever survives it.

What This Means For You

If you buy a domain listed here, you're not just buying a string of letters — you're buying the result of a screening process that's already ruled out the most common reasons a domain purchase goes wrong: active trademark conflicts, spammy or adult ownership history, and prices that don't hold up against real comparable sales.
