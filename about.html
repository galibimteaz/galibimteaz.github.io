How I work

Every name goes through DIOS, the Domain Investing Operating System, my five-stage due-diligence process:

  1. Brainstorm & shortlist. I generate 15–20 candidate names around the niche, the category signal and the tone.
  2. Trademark & USPTO clearance. Every candidate is checked for existing conflicts.
  3. Wayback Machine history audit. I rule out spam history, adult content and brand damage left by a previous owner.
  4. Comparable-sales valuation. I benchmark a fair price against real sales data from NameBio.
  5. Radio test & final decision. I say the name aloud to test pronunciation, spelling and 24-hour memorability.

A name that fails any stage is dropped, no matter how good it sounds. See it in action in the Juronix.com case study, where more than 40 names were rejected before one was approved.
