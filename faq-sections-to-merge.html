LinkedIn Ghostwriting

What does a LinkedIn ghostwriter actually do?

I write your LinkedIn posts in your voice, reply to comments and messages, and keep your profile active. You approve the posts before they go live, so nothing goes out that you haven't seen.

Who is this for?

Founders, brokers and domain investors who know LinkedIn matters but don't have time to post. If your last post was months ago, this is for you.

Will the posts sound like me?

That's the goal. We start with a short call about how you talk and what you care about. I also write one free sample post first, so you can judge the voice before paying for anything.

Why a ghostwriter who works in domains?

I'm a domain broker and investor myself. I already know the terms, the audience and what people in this space respond to, so you won't spend time explaining basics.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at $199 a month for 8 posts. Growth is $349 a month for 12 posts plus daily engagement, and Full Presence is $549 a month for 16 posts plus inbox handling. The first three clients get founding rates of $149, $279 and $449. A single post is $39.

How does the free sample work?

Message me with a few lines about your work. I write one post and send it back. If you like it, we talk about a plan. If not, you keep the post and we part as friends.

How long before I see results?

LinkedIn rewards consistency, so expect to post for 6 to 8 weeks before the pattern shows. I can't promise followers or leads, but a regular, clear presence makes it easier for people to find and trust you.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are monthly, with no long contract.

Domain Naming & Brokerage

What does a custom naming consultation include?

You get a shortlist of 15 to 20 brandable name candidates for your startup, each checked for trademark and USPTO conflicts and for availability. It costs $150 to $500.

How much does a domain from the portfolio cost?

Brandable domains from the current portfolio are priced from $500 to $5,000. Each one has a trademark check and a clean ownership history, and the price is benchmarked against comparable sales.

How does domain acquisition brokerage work?

If you want a domain that someone else owns, I identify the owner, negotiate the purchase and close the deal. The fee is a 10 to 20 percent commission.

What is a brand and domain audit?

It is a review of your current domain or brand name that covers trademark risk, credibility and growth-readiness, ending with a written recommendation. It costs $100 to $300.

What is DIOS?

DIOS stands for Domain Investing Operating System. It is the process every engagement runs through: trademark clearance, a historical audit and a comparable-sales valuation.

How is a domain transferred safely?

Domain purchases can go through a secure escrow transfer, and I offer follow-up support after the handoff.
