Why This Domain Sits at $3,000–$4,500, Not $10,000

Most sellers price a domain by feeling. Here's the actual math behind pricing Juronix.com — using real comps instead of gut instinct.

Most sellers price a domain by feeling. I price it by comps — here's the actual math.

Juronix.com is clean, brandable, and has no trademark conflicts. A typical seller would list it at $8,000–$10,000 because it "sounds premium." Here's what the data actually says.

What the comps show

NameBio comps for similar suffix patterns reflect real closed sales, not asking prices. Comparing negotiated Sedo sales against the GoDaddy auction floor shows the same pattern repeating — a 7–8x price gap depending on the venue a domain sells through.

Trademark clearance was verified, not assumed — a full search across relevant classes came back clean.

The realistic range

Based on that data, the realistic range for Juronix.com is $3,000–$4,500. Not because the name is weak, but because that's what the market has actually paid for names like it — verified, not guessed.

A price without data is just an opinion

Sellers: when's the last time you priced a domain off real comps instead of gut feeling? Comps decide the number. Not the vibe.
