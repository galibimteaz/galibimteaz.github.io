The Objective

Find a .COM domain built specifically for a legal-tech startup — short, credible, easy to pronounce, suitable for a global brand, and completely free of trademark risk. Most candidate names failed on at least one of these before they ever reached the shortlist.

The Due-Diligence Workflow

1
Brainstorm & Shortlist
Generate 15–20 candidate names built around the target niche (LegalTech), category signal, and tone.
Output: Raw candidate list
2
Trademark & USPTO Clearance
Every candidate checked against Trademark and USPTO TESS for existing conflicts.
✕ 22 names rejected — active trademark conflicts
3
Wayback Machine History Audit
Removed any candidate with old spam history, adult content, or former ownership issues.
✕ 7 names rejected — unclean history
4
Comparable-Sales Valuation
NameBio data used to benchmark fair value against similar LegalTech/brandable sales.
✕ 4 names rejected — mispriced for real range
5
Radio Test & Final Decision
Final candidates spoken aloud to test pronunciation, spelling, and 24-hour memorability.
✓ Juronix.com approved — added to portfolio
Only the strongest names make it through. Juronix.com passed every test.
