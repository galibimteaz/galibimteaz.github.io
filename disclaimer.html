2. What “trademark-cleared” means

When I describe a domain as trademark-cleared, I mean that at the time of my review I searched public trademark records (such as the USPTO TESS database, and in some cases EUIPO) and found no conflicting mark. This is a screening step, not a legal opinion.

  • Trademark rights differ by country, by industry and by the goods or services involved, and they change over time.
  • A search of public records can miss unregistered or pending rights.
  • Before you build a brand on any name, ask a trademark attorney in the countries where you will operate to confirm it is safe to use.
