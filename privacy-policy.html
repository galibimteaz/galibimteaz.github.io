The short version

  • I collect very little: what you send me, and (only if you accept) anonymous visit statistics.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts when a page opens, so Google sees your IP address. No cookies are involved.
  • I don't sell, rent or trade your information, and I don't share it with anyone for their own purposes.
  • I use your information only for the reasons listed below. Nothing else.
  • There are no ads, no advertising cookies and no profiling on this site.
  • Google Analytics cookies are set only after you click Accept. You can change your mind any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer.

1. Who is responsible for your data

This website, Domain Decoded (galibimteaz.github.io), is run by me, Imteaz (Galib) Chowdhury, from Dhaka, Bangladesh. I decide how the information described here is used. You can reach me at galibsjournal@gmail.com.

2. What I collect

Information you send me. If you use the contact form, I receive your name, email address, subject and message. If you email me, message me on WhatsApp or LinkedIn, or ask for a free LinkedIn sample post, I receive whatever you choose to share, such as your business details and goals.

Visit statistics (only if you accept). If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics records which pages you view, roughly where you are (country or city level), your device type, browser and how you arrived at the site. I do not collect your name or email through analytics, and I have turned off Google's advertising and personalization features.

Technical data from hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when your browser requests a page. I don't have access to a log of individual visitors.

Fonts from Google Fonts. The site's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (Google LLC). When a page opens, your browser requests the font files directly from Google, so Google receives your IP address and basic browser information. This happens even before you choose on the cookie banner, because the fonts are needed to display the page. Google states that these requests do not set cookies, and I don't receive this data.

Choices stored in your browser. The site remembers whether you accepted or declined cookies. These are stored locally on your device. See the table below.

4. How I use your information

  • To reply to you and deliver what you ask for. Messages and emails are used to answer your questions, give quotes, send a free sample post and carry out a service you request. (Legal basis: taking steps at your request, or my legitimate interest in running my business.)
  • To understand how the site is used. Visit statistics help me see which pages are useful and fix what isn't. (Legal basis: your consent.)
  • To display the site properly. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts so pages look as designed. (Legal basis: my legitimate interest in running a well-presented website.)
  • To keep records and meet legal duties. For example, invoices and payment records for work you have paid for.

I will not use your information for any other purpose, and I will not sell it or disclose it to anyone else, except where the law requires me to or where it is needed to carry out the service you asked for (see the next section).

5. Who else handles your data

I use a few trusted services to run the site and my work. They handle data only for the purposes below:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC): visit statistics, only after you accept. Google's privacy policy.
  • Formspree: delivers contact-form messages to my email inbox. Formspree's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC): delivers the site's fonts to your browser, as described in section 2. Google Fonts privacy information.
  • Email, WhatsApp and LinkedIn: if you contact me through them, your message is also handled under their own terms.
  • Payment providers and banks: if you pay me by Payoneer or bank transfer, the provider processes your payment details. I never see or store your card number.
  • Marketplaces and escrow services: if you click Buy Now on a domain, you go to a third-party page (for example mydomaincontact.com) where that provider's policy applies.

None of these may use your information for their own advertising because of anything I send them.

6. International transfers

I am in Bangladesh, and the services above may process data in other countries, including the United States. By visiting the site, contacting me or accepting analytics you understand your information may be handled outside your own country.

7. How long I keep it

  • Messages and emails: as long as needed to answer you, deliver any service and keep business records. I delete them when you ask, unless I must keep them by law.
  • Analytics data: for the period set in my Google Analytics account, then it is removed automatically.
  • Your cookie choice: until you clear your browser data or change it.

  • have your information deleted;
  • withdraw your consent for analytics at any time, using Cookie settings in the footer;

  • complain to your local data-protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email galibsjournal@gmail.com. I'll respond as soon as I can.
